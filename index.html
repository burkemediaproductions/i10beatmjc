Community Focused

It happens closer to home than many people realize.

Presentation materials provided by I-10 BEAT MJC highlight trafficking cases and task-force activity in Riverside County and the Coachella Valley. The message is direct: do not assume exploitation only happens somewhere else.

  • Learn red flags without stereotyping victims or survivors.
  • Understand why someone may not identify as a victim or may feel unable to leave.
  • Recognize trafficking as a business built on control, coercion, vulnerability, and profit.
  • Know who to contact when something does not look right.

Frequently Asked

Start with clear answers.

What is human trafficking?

Human trafficking involves the use of force, fraud, or coercion to obtain labor or a commercial sex act. When a minor is involved in commercial sex, force, fraud, or coercion is not required for the situation to be considered trafficking.

Who can book an I-10 BEAT MJC presentation?

Churches, schools, civic organizations, businesses, community groups, and families can request a presentation focused on trafficking awareness, warning signs, and practical response.

What should I do if someone is in immediate danger?

Call 911 if someone is in immediate danger. For suspected human trafficking, the National Human Trafficking Hotline is available 24/7 at 1-888-373-7888 or by text at 233733 .
