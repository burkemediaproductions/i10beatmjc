Book a Presentation

Bring practical, survivor-centered human trafficking awareness to your church, school, business, civic organization, or community group.

Community Education

Give your audience tools they can use.

I-10 BEAT MJC presentations are designed to help people move past stereotypes and understand how trafficking can actually appear in communities and workplaces.

  • What human trafficking is—and what it is not.
  • Sex trafficking and labor trafficking awareness.
  • Warning signs, vulnerabilities, and control tactics.
  • Commercial sexual exploitation of children (CSEC) indicators.
  • Why victims and survivors may not identify themselves as victims.
  • Safe, practical reporting and response guidance.
Good Fit For

Bring the conversation where people already gather.

Churches

Congregations, ministry teams, outreach groups, and faith-based service organizations.

Schools

Staff, parent groups, youth programs, and age-appropriate community education.

Businesses
