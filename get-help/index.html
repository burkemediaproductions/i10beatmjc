Get Help

If you are concerned about a trafficking situation, prioritize immediate safety and connect with trained professionals.

Immediate Danger

Call 911.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact emergency services first. Do not confront a suspected trafficker or take action that could increase risk.

Call 911
National Hotline

1-888-373-7888

The National Human Trafficking Hotline is available 24/7. You can also text 233733. Support is available in more than 200 languages.

Call HotlineText 233733
Before You Act

Safety matters more than proving what you saw.

  • Trust your instincts when a situation feels wrong, but avoid assumptions based on appearance alone.
  • Observe details you can safely remember without escalating the situation.
  • Do not attempt a rescue yourself or confront a suspected trafficker.
  • If no minor or imminent danger is involved, consider the National Human Trafficking Hotline or local non-emergency law enforcement.
  • Let trained advocates and appropriate authorities help determine next steps.
