Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use this website.

Privacy Policy

I-10 B.E.A.T M.J.C. respects the privacy of website visitors. This policy explains the basic types of information this website may receive through forms, analytics, donations, and normal website operation.

Information you provide

When you submit a contact, presentation, or help request form, the information you enter may be transmitted to I-10 BEAT MJC and its website service providers for the purpose of responding to your request.

Donations

Online donations are processed by a third-party payment provider. I-10 BEAT MJC does not need to store full payment-card details on this website.

Analytics and technical data

The site may use privacy-conscious analytics or standard server logs to understand traffic, performance, and website use. Technical data can include browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, and pages visited.

Sharing

Information is not sold. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, process a donation, respond to a request, prevent abuse, or comply with applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to scooteri10beat@gmail.com.
