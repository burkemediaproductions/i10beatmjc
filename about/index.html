I-10 BEAT MJC

About the Ministry

A faith-led biker ministry serving communities through human trafficking education, awareness, collaboration, and practical action.

Our Purpose

Driven by a mission greater than ourselves.

I-10 B.E.A.T M.J.C. stands for Biker Enthusiast Against Trafficking, Ministry of Jesus Christ. We believe awareness, education, service, and collaboration can help communities recognize exploitation and respond with greater wisdom and compassion.

Our work is informed by the experiences of victims and survivors. We aim to honor their dignity and resilience, stand alongside those most affected by modern slavery, and help communities move from assumptions to informed action.

Our Values

How we approach the work.

S

Service

We approach our work with humility and a spirit of service, recognizing that meaningful change requires a community working together.

D

Dignity

We honor the dignity and resilience of those affected by slavery and center the experiences of victims and survivors in our efforts.

E

Equity

We engage diverse perspectives and recognize structural inequities that can contribute to trafficking and modern slavery.

C

Courage

We value inquiry, innovation, resilience, and continuous learning—even when better solutions challenge what is typical or traditional.

Partnership

This work cannot be accomplished alone.

I-10 BEAT MJC actively pursues collaborations and partnerships that broaden the reach of trafficking education and awareness.

Churches, schools, businesses, civic groups, public agencies, community organizations, and individuals all have a role in recognizing warning signs, sharing trustworthy information, and connecting people to qualified help.
