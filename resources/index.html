Resources

Practical information for recognizing trafficking, responding safely, and connecting people with qualified support.

Remember the Basics

Believe. Know. Think. Stay informed. Report.

1

Believe

Human trafficking is real and can occur in ordinary communities, workplaces, relationships, and industries.

2

Know

Learn red flags, understand control and vulnerability, and avoid relying on movie-style stereotypes.

3

Think

Pay attention to how goods and services are produced, how workers are treated, and what circumstances surround exploitation.

4

Report

Keep trusted reporting resources available and choose a response that prioritizes immediate safety.

Trusted Help

National Human Trafficking Hotline

For suspected human trafficking or help leaving a trafficking situation, trained advocates are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Call: 1-888-373-7888
Text: 233733

If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

For Organizations

Use the website as a starting point—then bring the conversation into the room.

Churches & Ministries

Equip staff, volunteers, and congregations to recognize vulnerabilities, respond without judgment, and know where qualified help is available.

Schools & Youth Groups

Build age-appropriate awareness around grooming, online risks, commercial sexual exploitation of children, safe adults, and reporting concerns.

Businesses & Civic Groups

Train people who may see warning signs in hospitality, retail, transportation, service industries, neighborhoods, and public spaces.
